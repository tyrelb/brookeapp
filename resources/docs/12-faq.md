## Frequently asked questions

**A client's balance is negative. What happened?**
A pay-as-you-go client trained more than they had deposited, or a monthly member's fee has been posted and not yet paid. Record the payment when it arrives and the balance returns to zero or above. The dashboard lists everyone in this state.

**Why does a session need a gym?**
So the Gym usage report can reconcile every session against the gym's invoice. If you train at one gym it is filled in automatically. If you do not pay a gym per session, add the gym anyway with "Monthly rate only" or skip gyms entirely; the field only appears once you have more than one.

**How do I move a client to a different plan?**
Open the client, click **Edit**, choose the new plan and save. Sessions from then on use the new rates; sessions already charged are unchanged.

**I logged a session wrongly.**
Open it and click **Reopen**. Every charge is voided, you fix the attendance, and **Complete & charge** posts it again. Do not void the session charges from the ledger; the app will point you to Reopen instead.

**I recorded a payment wrongly.**
Click **Void** on that row in the client's ledger, then record it again correctly. Voided rows stay visible, struck through.

**Which GST figure do I remit?**
The monthly and annual reports show both. *Accrual* is GST on what you charged; *cash* is the GST inside what you received. They differ because of prepaid wallet deposits. Your accountant will tell you which basis you file on.

**When are monthly fees posted?**
Each morning at 6:00 Pacific time, for every active monthly member whose billing day has arrived, and never twice in the same month. **Post monthly fee** on the client page does the same thing on demand. Fees are not posted for months before the client's "Client since" date.

**Can clients pay by card?**
Not yet. Cash, cheque and e-Transfer are supported. Card payments are planned.

**Can a client book their own session?**
No. The invite, receipt and wallet page all tell clients to contact you, using the booking instructions from Settings → Business. Only you can book, move or cancel.

**A client's wallet link was forwarded to someone else.**
Open the client and click **Reset link**. The old link stops working immediately.

**What happens if my account is suspended?**
You are logged out and cannot log in, and your clients' wallet links stop working, until an administrator reinstates the account. Your data is kept.

## Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Fitness Wallet** | A client's prepaid balance. Deposits go in; each session takes its share plus GST out. |
| **Plan** | How a client pays: pay-as-you-go with a rate grid, a family plan sharing one wallet, or a monthly membership with a flat fee. |
| **Family plan** | One client record for a household, with up to ten named members on a single Fitness Wallet. Each member who attends a session is charged for it. |
| **Member** | One person inside a family client. Members are names only: they share the household's email, wallet link and balance. |
| **Service** | Something you offer, such as a 60-minute personal training session, with a default length. |
| **Tier** | The group size used to pick the rate: Single (1), Partner (2), Triple (3) or Quad (4). Larger groups are named by their size and pay the Quad rate. |
| **Rate grid** | A plan's price per person for each service at each tier, before GST. |
| **Log** | Record a session that has already happened and charge for it. |
| **Book** | Put a future session on the calendar, optionally with calendar invites. |
| **Repeat** | A booking that creates one session per date on chosen weekdays until an end date. |
| **Reopen** | Void a completed session's charges so attendance can be corrected and charged again. |
| **Void** | Strike a ledger row out so it no longer counts, while keeping it visible. |
| **Ledger** | The complete list of a client's payments, charges, fees, adjustments and refunds. |
| **Accrual basis** | GST counted when it is charged. |
| **Cash basis** | GST counted when the money is received. |
| **Wallet link** | A client's private, password-free page showing their balance, bookings and history. |
| **Finalize** | Lock a gym's month so the usage report matches the invoice you paid. |
