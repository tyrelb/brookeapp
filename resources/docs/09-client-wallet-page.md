Each client has a private web page that shows their side of the story: balance, plan, how to pay, upcoming bookings, and every deposit and session. There is no password. The address itself is the key, which is why the app calls it a link rather than an account.

## What the client sees

![The client's Fitness Wallet page, list view](/images/docs/portal-list.png)
*Ava's page. The balance is followed by a plain-English reading of it, such as "about 5 single sessions left". For a family it counts whole-family sessions, priced as though every member attends, so the number is never optimistic.*

- **Fitness Wallet balance** (or **Account balance** for a member) with a sentence explaining what it means.
- **Your plan** and how it bills, and **How to pay** with your accepted methods and e-Transfer address.
- **Book or change a session** repeats that only you can book, move or cancel, with your booking instructions, phone and email.
- **Upcoming sessions**, then **Activity** (deposits and charges) and **Training history** (sessions, with a "Missed" badge for no-shows). Long lists are split into pages.

Switch to **Calendar** with the buttons beside "Your sessions" to see the month at a glance, with arrows for previous and next months.

## Sharing the link safely

- Send it with **Copy link** or **Email link** from the client's page. It is also inside every invite and receipt, so most clients will already have it.
- Ask clients not to forward it. The page says so at the bottom.
- If a client thinks someone else has it, click **Reset link** on their page. The old address stops working immediately and the new one is included in their next email.

> **Note:** The page is read-only. Clients cannot book, cancel, or change anything, and nothing on it can be used to reach your other clients. Links are long random codes, the page is hidden from search engines, and it stops working if your account is suspended.
