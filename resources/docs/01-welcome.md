BrookeApp keeps the money side of personal training in one place: who your clients are, what each of them pays, which sessions happened, what you are owed, and what you owe the gym. It replaces the notebook, the spreadsheet and the "did she pay me for last Tuesday?" text messages.

## Two ways a client can pay

Every client is on a **plan**, and a plan works in one of two ways.

| | Pay-as-you-go (Fitness Wallet) | Monthly membership |
|---|---|---|
| **How it works** | The client deposits money into a Fitness Wallet, like topping up a transit card. Each session deducts a per-person rate plus GST. | A flat fee plus GST is posted to the client's account every month. Every session is included. |
| **What you watch** | The wallet balance. When it runs low, ask for a top-up. | Whether the monthly fee has been paid. |
| **Good for** | Clients who train irregularly or in small groups. | Regulars who train often and prefer one predictable bill. |

## How money flows

1. **You set up your rates once.** A **service** is something you offer, such as "Personal Training (60 min)". A **plan** holds the price of each service per person, for one, two, three or four people training together.
2. **A client pays you** by cash, cheque or e-Transfer. You record it on their page, and it lands in their Fitness Wallet (or pays down a monthly fee).
3. **You log a session** and tick who came. BrookeApp works out the rate from how many people showed up and deducts each person's share, plus GST, from their wallet.
4. **Reports add it up** by month and by tax year, with GST shown both as charged and as received, so you and your accountant can pick the basis you remit on.

> **Note:** Rates are always entered *before* GST, and GST (5% by default) is added on top. Money you receive is treated as GST-inclusive. You never have to do the tax arithmetic yourself.

## A tour of the dashboard

The dashboard is the first thing you see after logging in. It answers "how is this month going?" and "who needs my attention?" at a glance.

![The dashboard for the demo trainer, Brooke Fitness](/docs/images/dashboard.png)
*The dashboard. The three buttons at the top right are the actions you will use most: Add client, Book session and Log session.*

- **The four cards** show active clients, sessions completed this month, revenue before GST (with the GST charged underneath) and payments received this month.
- **Fitness Wallets running low** lists pay-as-you-go clients who no longer have enough credit for one more single session. A red number is a balance owing.
- **Monthly members with a balance owing** lists members whose fee has been posted but not paid.
- **Recent activity** is the latest entries across every client's ledger, and **Upcoming scheduled sessions** shows the next few bookings from your calendar.

## Finding your way around

The sidebar on the left is the same on every page:

- **Training**: Dashboard, Clients, Calendar and Sessions. This is where you spend most of your time.
- **Pricing**: Plans and Services. Set these up once, then rarely touch them.
- **Reports**: Monthly report, Annual report and Gym usage.
- **Your name** at the bottom opens a menu with Settings, this Documentation, and Log Out.

![The user menu at the bottom of the sidebar, with Documentation highlighted](/docs/images/menu-documentation.png)
*Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar to open this guide, or to reach Settings.*
